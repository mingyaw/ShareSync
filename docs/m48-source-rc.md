# M48 Product UX And Accessibility Closure

Status: Complete source-code checkpoint.

Android centers on providing photos and pairing; iOS centers on pairing and a
single sync-all action. Activity, diagnostics, destructive controls, and manual
endpoint recovery live outside the primary path. Both apps include Traditional
Chinese and English copy, appearance-aware semantics, accessibility labels,
adaptive layouts, progress feedback, first-run guidance, and explicit privacy
boundaries.
